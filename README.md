## What is CineDB?

CineDB is a full-featured **movie and TV series discovery platform** built as a passion project. It brings together everything a film enthusiast needs — browse the latest trending titles, dive deep into cast and crew, manage a personal watchlist synced across devices, leave reviews, and watch via embedded players.

It's designed to feel premium — fast, dark, cinematic.

---

## ✨ Features at a Glance

### 🔍 Real-Time Search
Search across **millions of movies, TV series, and people** instantly. Results update as you type, powered by the TMDB API. Filter by type — movies, TV shows, or cast members.

---

### 🎬 Full Detail Pages
Every title gets a dedicated page packed with:
- **Hero banner** with backdrop, poster, rating, and genre tags
- **Cast & crew** with clickable actor profiles
- **Trailers** embedded directly from YouTube
- **Streaming availability** by country
- **Community reviews** with star ratings and distribution chart
- **Related recommendations**

---

### 📋 Personal Watchlist
Add any movie or TV series to your watchlist with one click. Your list is:
- **Synced to your account** via Firebase Firestore
- **Available across all devices** when logged in
- Sortable and filterable — by genre, rating, type

---

### ⭐ Reviews & Ratings
Write full reviews for any title. The system includes:
- **Star ratings** (1–5)
- **Community rating distribution** chart
- Edit and delete your own reviews
- Reviews tied to your verified account

---

### 🎭 Actor & Director Profiles
Click any cast member to visit their full profile:
- Biography and personal details
- Complete filmography with posters
- Known for section

---

### 📺 CineDB Stream Console
Watch movies and TV episodes directly within the app via embedded third-party players:
- Multiple source options
- Season and episode selector for TV series
- Fullscreen support

---

### 🔐 Authentication System
Secure, full-featured auth powered by **Firebase Authentication**:
- Register with email & password
- **Mandatory email verification** before accessing protected features
- Login with session persistence
- Forgot password flow with email reset
- Account deletion — permanently wipes all your data

---

### 👤 User Profile
Your personal hub inside CineDB:
- Edit display name
- View watchlist statistics
- Manage account settings
- Permanently delete your account and all associated data

---

### 🏷️ Browse by Category
Explore curated content across sections:
- 🔥 Trending Today
- ⭐ Top Rated
- 🎬 Now Playing
- 📅 Upcoming
- 📺 Popular on TV
- Genre-based browsing (Action, Horror, Comedy, Sci-Fi, and more)

---

### 🌙 Dark / Light Mode
Full theme support with:
- System preference auto-detection on first visit
- Manual toggle in navbar
- Preference saved to localStorage — persists across sessions

---

### 📱 Fully Responsive
Built **mobile-first** from the ground up:
- Custom bottom navigation bar on mobile
- Fluid layouts across all screen sizes
- Touch-optimised interactions

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & Legal

CineDB takes user data seriously:
- Only collects what's necessary (email, display name, watchlist, reviews)
- No advertising, no data selling
- Full account deletion available at any time
- Firestore security rules restrict each user to their own data only
